GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

Some quick answers before you send an enquiry.

How do I request a service?+

Use the enquiry form below or email us with your requirements. We'll discuss the scope and quotation with you.

How is pricing decided?+

Pricing is custom. It depends on the service, project scope, complexity and agreed deliverables. A quote is shared after discussion.

What are the payment terms?+

The planned payment structure is 50% advance before work begins and the remaining 50% after completion, before final delivery. Confirm the terms in writing before paying.

Can I request changes?+

Revision limits will be agreed individually for each service and stated in the project quotation.

Are paintings available now?+

Not yet. Black-and-white paintings are planned for a later launch, initially with delivery limited to Bhubaneswar.

How long will my project take?+

We'll provide an estimated timeline after understanding your requirements and confirming the scope.
